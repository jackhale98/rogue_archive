# NetHack port — license and provenance report

This report covers every file the NetHack port distributes from the public site. It is an engineering record, not legal advice. Re-check it whenever the pinned upstream version changes.

**Summary:** everything shipped has a documented redistribution basis. NetHack's code, its data files and its tiles are under the **NetHack General Public License** (NGPL; `dat/license`), © Stichting Mathematisch Centrum and M. Stephenson and the NetHack DevTeam. Lua, which NetHack embeds, is MIT. The port's own files use the NGPL, as part of a NetHack distribution.

## Obligations and how they are met

The NGPL lets anyone copy and distribute NetHack in source or executable form, provided the copyright notice and the license come with it, and modified versions are distributed under the same terms with the source available (`dat/license`, sections 1–3).

- **License and notices:** the license is shipped as `licenses/NetHack-General-Public-License.txt` inside the runtime directory, and the game shows its own copyright banner at start-up, unchanged.
- **Source availability:** the deployed site links to this repository, which pins the exact upstream commit and contains the patch, the port's own source and the build scripts; anyone can rebuild the shipped files (`scripts/build.sh`).
- **Modified versions:** the one patch is in `patches/` with its reason; the port's files say they are under the NGPL.
- **Lua's MIT licence:** its notice is shipped.

## Provenance

NetHack is developed by the NetHack DevTeam, descending from Hack (Jay Fenlason, Andries Brouwer) and Rogue. The pinned tag `NetHack-5.0.0_Released` is upstream's latest release at the time of porting (May 2026).
